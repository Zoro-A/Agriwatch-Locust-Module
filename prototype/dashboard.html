Outcome colours: amber = locust recorded, dark green = survey found none, grey = unknown (not scored). Risk maps: darker red = higher forecast risk.

Model card. ConvLSTM (2 layers 32/64, 3x3, three horizon heads, 265,539+ parameters); seed 1; inputs: 6 anomaly channels + 3 recent-sightings channels + month (11 channels × 12 steps); trained on labels that had ended by 2020-10-15; validation PR-AUC at that refit 0.504. Replay origins show the forecast the rolling protocol made at that time, using only labels known then. The live origin runs the final exported model on the newest input window, which has no recorded positives yet.
Read with care. Rolling-origin evaluation of this model family did not clearly beat the simple persistence rule (see the results table in docs/RESULTS.md). The maps are a research prototype, not an operational warning. Per-origin PR-AUC values come from a single forecast and are indicative only. Grey cells in the outcome panel are unknown, not negative.
